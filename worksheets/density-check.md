# Check a density boundary

The CSV transcribes the standard scale shown on Freight Class Desk’s freight class chart, which identifies Supplement 1 to NMF 100-AZ, effective May 23, 2026.

Measure the packed unit in inches. Divide length × width × height by 1,728 to get cubic feet. Divide gross packed weight in pounds by cubic feet to get pounds per cubic foot. Compare the unrounded density with the CSV limits.

| Packed size (in) | Gross weight (lb) | Volume (ft³) | Density (lb/ft³) | Standard-scale class |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 48 × 40 × 45 | 1124.95 | 50 | 22.499 | 70 |
| 48 × 40 × 45 | 1125.00 | 50 | 22.500 | 65 |

Match the commodity to its current NMFC item and subitem before using the scale. Confirm that the provision applies to the packed shipment. Use the provision’s assigned class if it specifies one.
