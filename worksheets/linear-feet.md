# Estimate LTL linear feet

For identical rectangular pallets, measure the packed width across the trailer and the packed depth along it. Use inches throughout.

1. Pallets per row = floor(usable trailer width ÷ packed width). If that gives zero, that orientation does not fit.
2. Rows = ceil(pallet count ÷ pallets per row).
3. Linear feet = rows × packed depth ÷ 12.

Example from the Freight Class Desk linear-feet calculator: three packed 48 × 40 in pallets and 100 in of usable width, with no added clearance. With 40 in across, two fit per row and two 48 in rows use 8 ft. Turn them so 48 in goes across. Two still fit per row, and two 40 in rows use 6⅔ ft.

Include overhang and account for required clearance. With mixed sizes, sketch the rows, sum their depths, and divide by 12.
