# Freight Density Reference

Reusable density bands, a reproducible boundary check, and a trailer-space worksheet for LTL shipping.

- **density-bands.csv** — Import the standard density scale into a spreadsheet. The lower bound is inclusive and the upper bound is exclusive. A blank upper bound means the interval is open-ended.
- **density-check.md** — Reproduce the class change at the 22.5 lb/ft³ boundary. Check that the applicable NMFC item and subitem use this scale before assigning class.
- **linear-feet.md** — Calculate floor space from packed pallet dimensions and usable trailer width.
