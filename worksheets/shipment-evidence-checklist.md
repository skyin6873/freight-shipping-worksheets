# Shipment evidence checklist

Editorial workflow aid; no carrier acceptance or classification is implied.

- [ ] Record shipment date, quote reference, bill of lading and carrier.
- [ ] Record each handling unit's outside packed length, width and height, including overhang.
- [ ] Record gross weight including pallet and packaging; identify the measurement source.
- [ ] Retain measurement photos, scale record and time of measurement.
- [ ] Calculate each volume separately, then divide total weight by total volume; do not average unit densities.
- [ ] Record the commodity description and independently confirmed NMFC item/subitem and governing version.
- [ ] Distinguish a density calculation from commodity classification and a carrier's pricing rule.
- [ ] Retain the original written quote, governing tariff/customer pricing and any updated booking.
- [ ] On a correction, request carrier weight/inspection records and identify which input or rule changed.
- [ ] Review minimum charges, discounts and accessorials separately; a matching density does not establish a correct invoice.
- [ ] Leave unknown fields marked unknown; retain the evidence and reviewer behind each decision.
